Privacy Policy

Effective: November 12, 2026

zdxsv.net ("zdxsv") is an unofficial online battle server for the PlayStation 2 game Mobile Suit Gundam: Gundam vs. Zeta Gundam. This page explains what information about you the zdxsv server, this website and PCSX2 for zdxsv handle.

This is a translation. If it differs from the Japanese version, the Japanese version applies.

1. Information we collect

Sign-in with Discord (PCSX2)

  • Your Discord user ID

When you sign in, zdxsv asks Discord only for the minimum permission needed to confirm your account. Your Discord password and email address are never passed to zdxsv. Discord also sends your username and avatar, but zdxsv does not store them.

Accounts and battles

  • Your game login ID, user ID, handle name and team name
  • When the account was created and when you last logged in
  • Battle statistics (battles, wins and losses, kills, etc.) and a record of each battle (opponents, mobile suits used, result)
  • Battle replays

Connection information

  • IP addresses (when the account was created, at your last login, and in server logs)
  • With PCSX2: information that identifies your PC

PCSX2 crash reports

When PCSX2 for zdxsv crashes, it sends a crash report to the developers. It contains part of the memory at the time of the crash, the settings file (PCSX2.ini), recent log lines, your OS type, a random installation ID and the sender's IP address. You can turn this off in Settings > zdxsv.

What we do not collect

Lobby chat messages are not stored.

2. How we use it

  • Signing you in, logging you in and providing online battles
  • Providing battle statistics, rankings and replays
  • Dealing with cheating and abuse (including restricting use)
  • Investigating problems and improving the service

3. Public information

  • The connection information on this website shows the user ID and handle name of players in the lobby and in battle
  • Battle statistics, battle records and replays may be published together with user IDs and handle names

4. Third-party services

zdxsv uses the following services. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

  • Discord: sign-in
  • Google Cloud: server logs, database backups and replay storage
  • Sentry: PCSX2 crash reports
  • GitHub: this website and distribution of PCSX2 for zdxsv

Except where required by law, we do not share your information with any other third party.

5. Retention

Accounts, battle statistics and replays are kept for as long as zdxsv is provided. Server logs are kept only as long as needed to investigate problems.

6. Disconnecting Discord

  • You can remove zdxsv at any time in Discord under User Settings > Authorized Apps. In PCSX2, Settings > zdxsv > "Sign Out" revokes that PC's sign-in on the server
  • To have your Discord user ID deleted from zdxsv, contact us in the support channel of our Discord server

7. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy when needed. Changes will be announced on this page.
